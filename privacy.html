Privacy and cookies

This page explains what this website collects and why. It covers this site only.

Booking a call

When you book a voice audit, the booking form is provided by our scheduling provider (Calendly or Cal.com). The details you enter there (such as your name, email and any profile link you share) are handled by that provider and shared with us so we can prepare for and run the call. Campaign tags in the page address (utm parameters) are passed to the booking form so we know which ad or link brought you here.

Ad and analytics cookies

With your permission only, this site loads measurement tags from Meta, LinkedIn, Google and X. They tell us which ads bring visitors and bookings, and help those platforms show our ads to relevant people. If you decline, none of these tags load and the site works the same.

Your choice is stored in your browser (local storage, key tp-consent). Clear your browser data for this site to choose again.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. Content you share during onboarding is used to write your content.

Your rights and contact

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us at the email address listed on the main page.
