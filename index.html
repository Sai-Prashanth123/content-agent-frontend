How the content agent works

Five steps, run for every client, every draft.

  1. Learn
  2. Codify
  3. Write
  4. Challenge
  5. Ship

  1. Learn

    We collect your posts, video transcripts and website, so the agent starts from what you have actually said.

  2. Codify

    Your voice is measured into a voiceprint and written into "law" files: identity, voice, receipts, quality bar and content plan.

  3. Write

    Drafts are built only from your real stories, numbers and frameworks, in structures proven on your own account.

  4. Challenge

    Linters, then independent critics for voice, hook and substance, then an editor-in-chief. One veto sends it back.

  5. Ship and learn

    You approve every post. Your edits are recorded and tune your voice for the next draft.
