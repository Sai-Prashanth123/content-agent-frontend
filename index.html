    FAQ

    Questions

    Will it sound like AI?

    Every draft is scored against a voiceprint measured from your own posts and checked by linters that block common machine-written patterns. Then separate critics review it for voice before you see it.

    How is this different from a ghostwriter or ChatGPT?

    A ghostwriter guesses your voice and ChatGPT writes like everyone else. The agent starts from your real posts, uses only stories and numbers you can stand behind, and has to pass a panel of critics before a draft reaches you.

    How much of my time does it take?

    A short kickoff so we learn your goals and your no-go topics, then a few minutes a week to review and approve drafts.

    Who approves each post?

    You do. Nothing is published without your approval, and every edit you make is recorded so the next draft needs fewer.

    How much does it cost?

    It depends on the platforms and how often you want to post. Pricing is agreed on the call, after the free audit.

    Do you publish for me?

    That depends on how you like to work. Publishing, scheduling and cadence are agreed on the call.

    What data do you use?

    Your public posts, your video transcripts, your website, and anything you share with us during onboarding. Every number in a draft traces back to one of those sources.

    Is my data kept private?

    Your knowledge base is built for your account and used to write your content. How your data is stored and handled is agreed on the call.
